# Promptly v0.9.0

Promptly is now prompt-scale first: a fresh roll is something you would keep on
every command, and the interest comes from material rather than size.

- About nine in ten automatic rolls are one to three rows, two most often. Taller
  art (horizons, contour maps, automaton runs, large networks) appears when you
  ask for it with `--height`, and occasionally at four or five rows.
- Five new generative engines each commit a prompt to one idea, with your
  username and directory as part of it: `field` (ridges, dissolves, gradient
  beds, horizons), `tiles` (quarter-arc chains, meanders, mazes, contours),
  `automaton` (cellular automaton strips), `growth` (vines and coral), and
  `plot` (wave traces, loops, ridgelines, harmonographs).
- Compact `surface` and `prompt` rolls arrange your text as flowing or stacked
  clusters and give the slack one texture: truecolor beds that dither in,
  ridges, dissolves, automaton slices, arc chains, waves, meanders, gradient
  elbows into the input line.
- Brackets, pills, and leaders hug the current directory as it changes length;
  artwork and the input column stay put. Networks stay within about half the
  width and grow from the text.
- Bare `promptly` rolls a few seeds of one engine and keeps the most coherent;
  galleries drop each engine's weakest candidates and keep tall specimens to
  their share. Rare punctuation and hex alphabets are now opt-in.

Removed: the classic `--style` layouts and recipes from Promptly 0.5 and earlier
(recipe versions 1–5). Prompts you exported from them are standalone and keep
working; only their recipes can no longer be reloaded. Version-6 and version-7
recipes render exactly as before.

Upgrade: `brew update && brew upgrade promptly`

Roll: `promptly --preview` · Browse: `promptly explore` · Keep one:
`source <(promptly --seed SEED)`

Standalone downloads are available for macOS and Linux, ARM64 and x86-64.
`SHA256SUMS` contains archive checksums. No Node or Bun installation is required;
previews and exported prompts use zsh 5.8+. Truecolor textures look best in a
terminal with 24-bit color.
